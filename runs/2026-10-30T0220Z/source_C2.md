A. Aviles, C. Gruber, O. Luongo, H. Quevedo, "Cosmography and constraints on the equation of
state of the Universe in various parametrizations", Phys. Rev. D 86, 123516 (2012),

Eq. (1)-(2): the scale factor is expanded in a Taylor series around the present time t0 up to
sixth order, a(t) = a0 [1 + ȧ(t0)(t − t0) + ... + (1/6!) a⁽⁶⁾(t0)(t − t0)⁶ + O((t − t0)⁷)],
with a0 = 1 identified "without loss of generality".

Eq. (3): "H ≡ (1/a) da/dt, q ≡ −(1/(aH²)) d²a/dt², j ≡ (1/(aH³)) d³a/dt³,
s ≡ (1/(aH⁴)) d⁴a/dt⁴, l ≡ (1/(aH⁵)) d⁵a/dt⁵, m ≡ (1/(aH⁶)) d⁶a/dt⁶."
"The subscript 0 indicates that the coefficients are evaluated at t = t0."

Text before Eq. (15), verbatim: "To evaluate the CS for ΛCDM we use Eqs. (4) with
H = H0 √(Ωm (1 + z)³ + 1 − Ωm), yielding"
(CS = cosmographic series, i.e. q0, j0, s0, l0, m0; z is the redshift, 1 + z = a0/a(t).)
