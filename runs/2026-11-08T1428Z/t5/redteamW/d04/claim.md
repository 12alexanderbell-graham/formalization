Reader would wrongly believe: the pinned phrase "the Hubble rate a'(t)/a(t) of the scale factor a at time t" describes expansionRate; the pin theorem shows any HubbleSpec-satisfying H equals it.
Why it is false: expansionRate is |a'/a|, the absolute value, so it differs from the Hubble rate by sign whenever a' < 0 (contracting universe has H < 0). The pin theorem hides this behind an extra hypothesis 0 <= deriv a t in its domain, which the generated claim does not show as a restriction on the pinned definition's phrase. A time-reversal theorem (expansionRate (a(-.)) t = expansionRate a (-t)) is then true and reads as the physical time reversal H -> -H.
