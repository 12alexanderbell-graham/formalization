# Screen 1628Z: quasar/GRB log-polynomial cosmography

Every printed formula below was read from a 200-dpi typeset crop in this folder (rendered from `arxiv.org/pdf/<id>`). The derivations start from first principles: d_L = (1+z)(c/H0) S_k(∫dz/E). For the log-polynomial, z = 10^x − 1 = e^u − 1 with u = ln 10 · x, so a_n = b_n · ln(10)^(n−1), where b_n is the coefficient of [ln(1+z)]^n. The general cosmographic series comes from a(t) = 1 + t − q0 t²/2 + j0 t³/6 + s0 t⁴/24 + l0 t⁵/120 by series reversion, with curvature included. As a check, it reduces to the flat-ΛCDM b_n when ΛCDM (q0, j0, s0, l0) are substituted.

Scripts (run `python3 -I <script>`; output is in `*.out.txt`):
- `derive_logpoly.py`: a_n(Ω_m) for n ≤ 5, general b_n(q0, j0, s0, l0, Ω_k), z-series D_L^n, and the printed-minus-derived checks.
- `check_numbers.py`: the Ω_m(a_n) inversions in Yang et al. Table 2, the wCDM intersection in the RL19 Fig. 3 caption, and the ΛCDM reference values.
- `check_DA_y.py`: the d_A z-series (checks Demianski 2017b) and the d_L y-series (checks Yang et al. (A.4)–(A.5)).

**ID note:** the brief gave arXiv:2004.09979 for Yang, Banerjee & Ó Colgáin (PRD 102, 123532), but that ID is Khadka & Ratra (MNRAS 2020). The correct paper is **arXiv:1911.01681**, and that is the one screened here. Demianski et al. 2017b (arXiv:1609.09631) was added because Lusso+19 Eqs. (2)–(7) cite it as their source.

ΛCDM reference values at Ω_m = 0.3, derived here: a2 = 2.9358, a3 = 3.5412, a4 = 1.1207, a5 = −1.8242.

## Bottom line
- **None of the relations behind the quoted ΛCDM-consistency tests is wrong.** Those relations are the a_n(Ω_m) in Lusso+19 Eq. (8), Bargiacchi+21 B.1/B.2, and Banerjee+21 Eqs. (2), (4), (6), plus RL19's plotted curve. The ~4σ claims and the critiques' counter-claims (8σ/6σ mock failures, Ω_m(a4) > 1) rest on correct algebra. The dispute is about truncation and convergence, not about the coefficients.
- **New formula misprints:** RL19 Methods a2, a3 and a3(a2) are printed without the ln10 powers, and a3 has Ω_M for Ω_M² (#1–2, not used). RL19 Fig. 3 caption has w = 1 where w = 0 is meant (#3). In the fifth-order z-series, D⁴_L has −105q0⁴ for +105q0⁴ (Demianski 2017b Eq. 17, copied into Lusso+19 Eq. 7). Demianski 2017b D⁴_A (Eq. 23) has +137j0 for −137j0. Yang+20 (A.5) prints C2 = 2.27652 for 2.27625.
- **Possible numerical consequence (unverified):** #6–8 enter (q0, j0, s0, l0) fits that print z-series coefficients but say they work in y. The papers alone cannot show whether the code used the printed z-series. l0 is reported as unconstrained in both papers, so any effect would mostly fall on l0.
- **Priority 2** (Capozziello, D'Agostino, Luongo 2019, arXiv:1904.01427) was **not screened** for lack of time.
