# Pilot of the expert-validation protocol (run 0828Z): adjudication

**This is a pilot, not THE RESULT part 4.** The reviewers were two subagents of a different model
(Sonnet), not independent human experts. Purpose: test that the packet is usable, that the planted
controls are detectable, and catch defects before experts see the packet.

Setup: packet generated by `validation/make_packet.py` from `validation/PROTOCOL.md` v1 (seed
`72c4d16f819f…`) on top of commit 0f72e7f (docstrings as they were before this run's fixes). 48
items (40 genuine, 8 planted) split by parity: reviewer A odd items, reviewer B even items. Each
reviewer saw only its copy of the items, `GLOSSARY.md` and `ANSWER_FORM.md` in /tmp (not the
repository); both recomputed claims with sympy/mpmath and read the cited arXiv PDFs. Answers:
`ANSWERS_A.md`, `ANSWERS_B.md`; their scripts: `scripts_A/`, `scripts_B/`.

## What the pilot says about the protocol

- The packet is self-sufficient: both reviewers could check every genuine item (Q3 CANNOT TELL only
  for items without an equation-level source: chemistry, pins).
- The planted defects are detectable by a careful reviewer; 8/8 suggests they may be too easy to
  discriminate expert diligence well. Kept for v1 (the threshold is a floor, not a score); a later
  round can add subtler controls.
- Minor defects cluster in prose (docstrings), never in statements, definitions or numbers: the
  mechanical checks cover the formal side; the prose side had no check until this run.
