Category: misleading-record
Reader would wrongly believe: that candidate C28 (Lusso et al. 2019, Sect. 3.2: "the logarithmic expansion matches the standard model ... below 2%" is false) is a verified finding, namely F11, with a Lean record behind it. The candidates row a reader scans for "what became of C28" is now `| C28 | — | — | → **F11** |`, exactly the shape FB22 blesses for a promoted candidate, and F11's title note now says "(was C15, C16, C28)".
Why it is false: C28 was never promoted. Its own (deleted) row said "Presentational ... Not formalized" and it is a floating-point comparison on two routes. F11's only record, `Checked.Records.F11_sizes`, is `RoundsTo (-210 * q₀^4) (-86) 0 ∧ RoundsTo (7/4 * q₀^4 * z^4) 0.72 2` at q₀ = -4/5, z = 1: two sizes about the D⁴_L sign error of Demianski et al. and nothing about a 2% accuracy claim, Taylor truncation or Ω_M ranges. FB22 binds a pointer row only to the ids in the title note of the finding, which is author prose under the unchecked marker; the author adds the id there (FB7 is satisfied by a neutral LEDGER line naming FINDINGS.md and `F11`).
Expected: ACCEPTED (FB22 bound by author-edited title note; FB7 satisfied by disclosure).
