# Answer

Main rule: STATE.md line 3 says its own statuses are hand-written and that the generated Status line in CLAIMS.md decides. All five have a generated Status line saying "proved in module ... of `Checked`", axioms `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`, `propext`, and "open fidelity objections on record: none".

STATE.md also has one-line bullets under three of the headings (not under lookback_second_order or avilesL0Claim_false). They say the statement is wrong: "sign of the pressure gradient term ... is the inverse one" (fs_neg_reversed), "the equation ... has the inverse sign of the exponent" (gelfand_extrema), "inverse order of the quantifiers" (many_centre). They are unchecked prose with no source or reasoning. They are not entered in the objection registry. The printed Lean statements contradict them:
- fs row: q' = -(f q) + 0.03(1-p^2), which is the Falkner-Skan equation at beta = -0.03.
- gelfand_extrema: w'' + (2/s)w' + e^w = 0.
- many_centre: the quantifier order is "for every N there is eps".

I am not certain they are meaningless. Anyone citing these should ask the colleague what the bullets refer to.

## 1. lookback_second_order
(a) yes.
(b) all, with hypotheses: a : R -> R, t0 : R, a differentiable near t0, deriv a differentiable at t0, a t0 != 0, deriv a t0 != 0. The conclusion is an asymptotic (little-o) statement as t -> t0.
(c) "**For every** `a : ℝ → ℝ`, `t₀ : ℝ`: **assuming** 1. `∀ᶠ (t : ℝ) in nhds t₀, DifferentiableAt ℝ a t` ..." (CLAIMS.md l.39-46). The Status line: "the statement is proved in module `Checked.Cosmography.Lookback` of `Checked`".

## 2. avilesL0Claim_false
(a) yes. It proves that a published formula is false.
(b) no parameters. The statement is "it is false that (`AvilesL0Claim`)", with no hypotheses. `AvilesL0Claim` is the universal claim "for every Omega, a, t0 (flat LCDM, C^6): cosmoCoeff 5 = 1 + 3*Omega - 27/2*Omega^2". So it only says the printed formula fails somewhere. The material lists `AvilesWitness.printedL0_false_at`, with hypotheses 0 < Omega < 1, as a separate theorem. I did not see where the printed formula fails, so I would not say more than "fails for some Omega". The correct value is 1 + 3*Omega + 27/2*Omega^2 (flatLCDM_coeffs).
(c) "**It holds that** - it is false that (`AvilesL0Claim`)" (CLAIMS.md l.115-117). Status line: "proved in module `Checked.Cosmography.LCDMCoefficients`".

## 3. gelfand_extrema
(a) yes.
(b) no free physical parameter. The equation is fixed as w'' + (2/s)w' + e^w = 0 (3-D ball, coefficient 2/s), with w(0) = 0 and regularity hypotheses. It quantifies over all solutions w, phi satisfying the 4 hypotheses. The conclusion gives specific numbers: zeta_0 in [4.071496844, 4.071496845], lambda* in [3.321992118, 3.321992119], and the oscillation of lambda about 2.
(c) "**For every** `w : ℝ → ℝ`, `φ : ℝ → ℝ`: **assuming** 1. for every `s`, if `s ∈ Set.Ici 0`, then `HasDerivWithinAt w (φ s)...`" ... "4. `w 0 = 0`" (CLAIMS.md l.6664-6671).

## 4. gelfand_pde_radial_many_centre
(a) yes. STATE.md says an earlier version of this theorem fell to the referee (it compared functions on the whole space, not just the ball) and was replaced by the "distinct at the centre" version. The listed one is the corrected one.
(b) particular, in three parts, for radial solutions of Laplacian u + lam e^u = 0 in the unit 3-ball with u = 0 on the sphere:
- (1) lam = 2 exactly (the equation is printed with 2*exp(u)): infinitely many solutions with distinct centre values.
- (2) for every N there is eps > 0 such that every lam with |lam - 2| < eps has N solutions with distinct centre values.
- (3) every lam with 2 <= lam < 3.321992118 has two solutions with distinct centre values.
Nothing is claimed for lam < 2 (outside the eps-neighbourhood) or for lam >= 3.321992118 here. The exact counts are in the separate gelfand_pde_radial_count.
(c) "3. for every `lam : ℝ`, if `2 ≤ lam`, then if `lam < 3.321992118`, then there is `u` ... such that there is `v` ... `u 0 ≠ v 0`" (CLAIMS.md l.6687). Parts 1 and 2 are l.6685 and l.6686.

## 5. fs_neg_reversed_wall_shear_3_exists
(a) yes.
(b) particular: beta = -3/100 = -0.03 only. This is the reversed-flow solution, with q(0) strictly between -0.0825629652 and -0.0825629651 (the "-825629652e-10 < q 0" literals). It is one row of the Ganapol 2010 Table 7. It is an existence claim, not uniqueness. STATE.md says uniqueness is "numerical only".
(c) "`HasDerivWithinAt q (-(f η * q η) - -3 / 100 * (1 - p η ^ 2)) (Set.Ici 0) η`" (CLAIMS.md l.6462). The blind-reader literal is `fun f p q η => -(f η * q η) - -3 / 100 * (1 - p η ^ 2)`.

## (d) Trust
I would trust the generated CLAIMS.md statements and Status lines for what Lean actually proved, since they are printed from Lean and CI-checked. I would not trust the hand-written STATE.md prose without checking, because its own header calls it unchecked, and it contains the unexplained "inverse" bullets above. Also, the fidelity record says explicitly that it does not prove the statements match their physics source, and it lists some literals as grandfathered or never read twice (for example the lookback and Aviles-type claims).
