## Caveat on three STATE.md bullets
STATE.md l.147, 211 and 218 contain hand-written sentences that read like objections:
 - "the sign of the pressure gradient term in the equation is the inverse of Falkner-Skan"
 - "the statement has the inverse order of the quantifiers to Gelfand"
 - "the equation in the statement has the inverse sign of the exponent to Gelfand"
These sit in STATE.md, which its own banner (l.3) calls unchecked prose. The generated fidelity records in CLAIMS.md list "open fidelity objections ... none" for all three. I did not find the cited sources, and I could not resolve the conflict from these two files. Checking the equations as printed in CLAIMS.md against the textbook forms, I found no sign inversion:
 - The standard Falkner-Skan form f''' + f f'' + beta(1 - f'^2) = 0 gives q' = -f q - beta(1 - p^2). With beta = -3/100 that is the printed `- -3/100 * (1 - p^2)`.
 - The Gelfand equation Laplacian u + lam e^u = 0 is also the standard one.
I treat the three bullets as unresolved, unchecked remarks that do not change the answers. They are a reason to read the statements themselves before citing.
