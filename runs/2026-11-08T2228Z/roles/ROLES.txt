Notes: Roles for t (hubble, decel, curv, cosmoCoeff) are deliberately generic since the specs only call it cosmic time. For FriedmannEq and MasterEq, F is the function of the Ricci scalar in the gravity action (De Felice-Tsujikawa style, minus convention), and t0 is the time at which the matter density parameter is defined. In Duffing, k multiplies the linear term and beta the cubic term of the restoring force. In the Michaelis-Menten spec, k1 is binding, km1 is unbinding (reverse), k2 is catalysis. In Robertson, k1 drives A to B, k2 the catalysed B plus C to A plus C, k3 the 2B to B plus C reaction. Nothing was undecidable.
