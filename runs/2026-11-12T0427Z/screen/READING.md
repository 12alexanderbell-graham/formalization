## Disagreements in the printed digits

- xi_max: 6.451 (Galli 2002, astro-ph/0408277) vs 6.45 (Sipila 2011/2015) vs 6.5 (Racca, Kandori, Kainulainen, Harvey, astro-ph/0303642, 0809.1457).
- rho_c/rho_0: 13.98 (Galli 2002) vs 14.04 (Curry & McKee) vs 14.1 (Sipila 2015, 2011.07849) vs 14 (Galli table, Racca, Kandori, 0809.1457, Kainulainen "~14"). Note 13.98 vs 14.04 vs 14.1 are three different roundings/values for what should be one number; 13.98 is the odd one out.
- m: 1.18 (most) vs 1.182 (Galli 2002) vs 1.1822 (Curry & McKee).
- R_BE coefficient (in the P_ext form c_s^2 G^{-1/2} P^{-1/2}): 0.48 (Larson) vs 0.485 (Curry & McKee) vs 0.49 (astro-ph/0408277). Not seen: 0.41 (the G M/c_s^2 form).
- Internal tension: astro-ph/0408277 prints 6.451 with R coefficient 0.49, while Curry & McKee print 0.485 and Larson 0.48; Sipila prints xi_0 = 6.45 yet contrast 14.1, whereas Galli pairs 6.451 with 13.98.
