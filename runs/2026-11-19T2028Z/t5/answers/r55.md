## 1. lookback_second_order
(a) Yes. Proved in `Checked.Cosmography.Lookback`; axioms are the standard three (propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound); open fidelity objections: none.
(b) All, with hypotheses. For every scale factor a and time t0, assume: a is differentiable near t0, deriv a is differentiable at t0, a t0 ≠ 0, and deriv a t0 ≠ 0. Then the second-order lookback-time expansion holds as a little-o of z^2. It has no cosmological parameters. Of the four hypotheses, 2 are shown needed and 2 redundant.
(c) CLAIMS.md line 37: "the statement is proved in module `Checked.Cosmography.Lookback` of `Checked`; axioms ... `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`, `propext`" (and "open fidelity objections on record ...: none").

## 3. gelfand_extrema
(a) Yes. Proved in `Checked.Math.GelfandExtrema`; 14 literals read blind; objections: none.
(b) No free physical parameters. The setting is the 3-D unit-ball radial Gelfand problem with the fixed nonlinearity e^w. It holds for every w, φ with 4 hypotheses: w' = φ on [0,∞), φ continuous at 0, φ' = −(2/s)φ − e^w for s > 0, and w(0) = 0. The conclusion is a turning-point sequence ζ with ζ0 in [4.071496844, 4.071496845] and λ(ζ0) in [3.321992118, 3.321992119]. λ(s) = s²e^{w(s)} has λ → 2.
(c) CLAIMS.md line 6649–6652 / 6656: "for every `s : ℝ`, if `s ∈ Set.Ici 0`, then `HasDerivWithinAt w (φ s) (Set.Ici 0) s`" ... "`w 0 = 0`". Line 6643: "the statement is proved in module `Checked.Math.GelfandExtrema` of `Checked`".
STATE.md line 214 says "the equation in the statement has the inverse sign of the exponent of the formula". The statement printed in CLAIMS.md (φ' = −(2/s)φ − e^w) is the standard w'' + (2/s)w' + e^w = 0 that STATE line 215 describes. The bullet does not match it.

## 4. gelfand_pde_radial_many_centre
(a) Yes. Proved in `Checked.Math.GelfandPDEManyCentre`; objections: none; 10 literals read blind. The first, flawed version (distinct as functions on the whole space) was dropped (STATE line 210).
(b) Particular. Radial classical solutions of Δu + λe^u = 0 in the 3-D unit ball with u = 0 on the sphere, distinct at the centre.
 - λ = 2: infinitely many (an injective sequence of centre values).
 - ∀N ∃ε > 0: for every λ with |λ−2| < ε there are N such solutions. ε depends on N, and its size is not given.
 - Every λ with 2 ≤ λ < 3.321992118: at least two solutions.
 It says nothing about λ < 2 or λ ≤ 0. It gives no exact counts here (that is `gelfand_pde_radial_count`).
(c) CLAIMS.md line 6668: "for every `lam : ℝ`, if `2 ≤ lam`, then if `lam < 3.321992118`, then there is `u` ... there is `v` ... (`u 0 ≠ v 0`)". Line 6667 gives the "for every `N`, there is `ε`" form.
STATE.md line 207 says "the statement has the inverse order of the quantifiers of the formula". The CLAIMS.md statement has the order ∀N ∃ε ∀λ, which is what STATE line 208 describes. The bullet does not match it.
