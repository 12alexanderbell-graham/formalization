Category: misleading-record (reaction mechanism permuted, roles kept)
Reader would wrongly believe: every Robertson line says "... the reaction network in which A turns into B with rate constant of the first reaction `k₁`, two B turn into B and C with rate constant of the second reaction `k₂`, and B and C turn into A and C with rate constant of the third reaction `k₃`" - i.e. k₂ is attached to 2B -> B + C and k₃ to B + C -> A + C.
Why it is false: in Robertson's problem (and in the Lean body: y₁' = -k₁y₁ + k₂y₂y₃, y₃' = k₃y₂²) k₂ belongs to B + C -> A + C and k₃ to 2B -> B + C. Any headline stating a property of a solution for particular k₂, k₃ values is mis-described (stiffness ratio, which reaction is fast).
Weakness used: P3 binds only the words immediately before the first occurrence of each placeholder ("rate constant of the second reaction {k₂}") and P2 only checks word membership; the reaction content (species words) between roles is a permutable block. The body names species only through y₁..y₃/k symbols; a reader must decode them through the phrase itself.
Expected: ACCEPTED (maybe DISCLOSED if the body's equations are decoded by hand); a P7 membership check passes (same multiset of words); only a species/reaction CLAUSE compared word for word would catch it.
