Reader would wrongly believe: what the reading guide at the top of FINDINGS.md now says about every verified finding: "*Printed*: … each with the blind second reading that confirms it against the source and the reader's own description of where the source prints it; *Correct*: the correct value or formula that replaces the published one, proved in Lean … Everything under **Unchecked notes** … is author prose that no machine check reads but that the finding's Referee line reports as re-read by an independent referee against the typeset source; a reader may rely on the generated part and on the title it quotes." A scientist who follows the guide therefore takes the generated lines as a Lean proof of the corrected formula with a source-confirmed printed value, and takes the author's title (the published-error claim) as referee-confirmed.
Why it is false: (1) the generated Correct lines state only the quantifier, class phrases and direction of the record's rounding (F8, F11: "the certified values below"; F4–F7: a difference of two numbers at one instance such as `printedD2y (decel Real.exp 0) …` below `correctD2y …`); no record proves the corrected formula of the source, e.g. `Checked.Records.F4_values` is `RoundsTo (printedD2y …) (-2.8333) 4 ∧ RoundsTo (correctD2y …) 2.8333 4 ∧ …`; the physics (`lumDist_y_third_order`) is cited only in the notes. (2) "confirms it against the source": the agreement theorems (`agree_printedD2y` …) show two transcriptions equal, not that either matches the source page, and for F3–F11 the Printed line itself says the reading predates the blind route and "the source is not described by a reader". (3) the claim that the notes were re-read against the typeset source is not something any check reads or the repository can show: each finding's Referee line is itself an unchecked note (a subagent's verdict, STANDS-WITH-CORRECTIONS etc.), and the marker line under every finding says that no check reads the notes; the guide turns a labelled author statement into an assurance.
