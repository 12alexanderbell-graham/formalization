Category: misleading-record
Reader would wrongly believe: that the verified finding F2 (CPL l₀ term, Capozziello et al.) holds for a matter density chosen freely. In the generated Correct line of F2 the toy record reads "`F2_toy` (for every data meeting its hypotheses; class: valid whichever matter density is chosen (Lean: `Ω := 245 / 1000`)): the certified values below", and the generated heading and the Correct line are the part the reading guide says to trust.
Why it is false: `Checked.Records.F2_toy` fixes the matter density parameter: its statement assumes `IsFlatCPL Ω w₀ wₐ a t₀` with `Ω := 245 / 1000`, `w₀ := -93 / 100`, `wₐ := -41 / 100` and concludes `RoundsTo (cosmoCoeff 5 a t₀) 0.809 3` and `RoundsTo (printedCLSL0 (245/1000) (-93/100) (-41/100)) (-0.906) 3`. At another Ω the certified values are different numbers; nothing is proved "whichever matter density is chosen". The new registry line `Ω := 245 / 1000 => valid whichever matter density is chosen` passes FB12 (the atom is an assumption of the universal statement) and FB15 (QUANT_WORD is a deny-list of seven words; "whichever" is not one of them, and every token of the phrase is an ordinary ASCII word, so the symbol rule is silent); it is the round-AN d02 move (a registry phrase that generalises a fixed parameter) with the quantifier spelled in a word the list lacks.
